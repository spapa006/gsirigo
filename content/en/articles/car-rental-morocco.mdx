Morocco is one of the most rewarding countries in the world to explore by car — the Atlas Mountains, the Sahara fringe near Merzouga, the Atlantic coast from Essaouira to Agadir, and the imperial cities of Marrakech, Fès and Rabat. Public transport connects the big cities, but it simply cannot touch the freedom of your own wheels. Here's how rental works on the ground.

## Costs and what to expect

Rental prices are generally **lower than Western Europe**, though they rise sharply in high season (spring holidays and October). Expect to pay for a compact car roughly the equivalent of €25–45 per day in mid-season, more for SUVs — which are genuinely useful if you plan mountain or desert detours. Fuel is competitively priced, and weekly deals usually offer the best value.

## Requirements at the desk

- **Age:** most suppliers require 21+, with surcharges under 25.
- **Licence:** bring your national licence; an **International Driving Permit** is required by most suppliers for non-Moroccan licences.
- **Deposit:** international credit card required; a pre-authorisation deposit is standard.
- **Documents:** your passport and the same card used to book.

## Insurance: read the Moroccan reality

Basic cover is included but always carries an excess. Are you planning to drive outside paved tourist routes? Then consider a plan with strong included protection — gravel tracks and river crossings are where rental cars actually get damaged in Morocco. Third-party excess protection bought in advance is usually far cheaper than upgrades sold at the counter.

## Driving tips

Morocco drives **on the right**. Expect a more dynamic traffic culture — particularly in medina-adjacent streets and rural roads where scooters, donkeys and pedestrians share the carriageway. Keep the tank topped up when you leave cities, carry water for desert stretches, and let your supplier know your planned route. On the plus side: magnificent scenery, light traffic outside urban areas, and some of the friendliest people you'll meet anywhere.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an International Driving Permit in Morocco?** Most suppliers require one for foreign licences. Arrange it before you travel.

**Is it safe to drive from Marrakech to the desert?** Yes, with planning: use main routes, refuel in larger towns, and avoid driving the Atlas passes after dark in winter.

**What is the minimum rental age in Morocco?** Usually 21, with a young-driver fee below 25. Some 4x4 categories require a higher age.
