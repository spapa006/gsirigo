Nobody "rents from a booking site" — you rent from an actual car hire company; the booking program is the middle layer that gives you access, comparison and customer support. Three programs consistently appear in the Travelpayouts car rental program: Rentalcars.com, AutoEurope and EconomyBookings. Here's how they actually differ.

## Rentalcars.com — the broadest inventory

Owned by Booking Holdings, Rentalcars aggregates a huge global inventory of suppliers and vehicles across thousands of locations. Its search results are fast, its filter set is deep, and its scale means you see the widest range of suppliers in one view. **Best for:** worldwide travel where you want maximum choice with a familiar, reliable interface.

## AutoEurope — the long-term and specialist option

AutoEurope has spent decades as a specialist in European rentals — particularly long-term, luxury and corporate travel — extending to worldwide destinations. If your itinerary involves a multi-week rental, a prestige vehicle, or specialist vehicle classes, AutoEurope's deeper specialist catalogue is worth checking first. **Best for:** European road trips, long-duration rentals and specialist vehicles.

## EconomyBookings — the budget-focused pick

EconomyBookings positions itself around affordable rates across a wide network of suppliers, with a strong presence for value-conscious travellers. Budget categories, last-minute dates and price-sensitive searches are its home turf. **Best for:** travellers optimising for the lowest headline price.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are all three programs reliable?** They are established, worldwide programs used by millions of travellers. What varies is inventory depth, pricing and the specific supplier shown for any given search.

**Will the same car cost the same everywhere?** No — the same vehicle class can differ substantially between programs because suppliers negotiate different rates with each. Always compare.

**Which is best for a long rental in Europe?** For multi-week or specialist rentals, AutoEurope is often the strongest first stop. Compare anyway — the difference can be significant.
