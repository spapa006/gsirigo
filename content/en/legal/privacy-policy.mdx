This Privacy Policy explains how Gsirigo ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website.

## Information we collect

- **Usage data** — anonymous, aggregated information about how visitors use the site (pages visited, device type, approximate region), collected through privacy-respecting analytics.
- **Information you submit** — details you send us through the contact form (name, email address, message content).
- **Booking information** — we do **not** collect or store any booking, payment or travel details. All searches and bookings are handled directly on our partners' websites under their own privacy policies.

## How we use your information

- To operate, maintain and improve the website.
- To respond to enquiries sent through the contact form.
- To measure site performance and content usefulness (in aggregate only).

## Cookies and analytics

Our site may use strictly necessary cookies for basic functionality. Where analytics are enabled, they use privacy-conscious measurement, and you can opt out at any time in your browser settings. We do not serve our own advertising cookies. Our partners' widgets may set cookies when you interact with them, governed by the partners' own cookie policies.

## Third-party partners

When you use a search widget on our site, you may be redirected to a partner website (Rentalcars.com, AutoEurope, EconomyBookings or another partner). Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

## Data retention

Contact-form messages are retained only as long as needed to respond, then deleted. Analytics data is retained in aggregate form only.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the contact page.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
