Gsirigo is an independent car rental comparison website. We are **not** a rental company: we do not operate a vehicle fleet, and we never handle bookings or payments directly.

## How we make money

When you search for a car through one of the search widgets or partner links on our site and complete a booking on a partner's website (Rentalcars.com, AutoEurope, EconomyBookings or another partner), the partner may pay us a commission. This commission **never changes the price you pay** — the price is set by the rental provider and is identical whether or not you reach it through Gsirigo.

## Transparency

Because our revenue depends on completed bookings — not on clicks, rankings or ad impressions — our interest is aligned with yours: helping you find a suitable car at a fair price. We aim for honest, up-to-date guides and comparisons. Editorial content is written independently, and affiliate relationships never determine which providers appear in our comparisons.

## Booking and payment

All searches, pricing, availability and bookings are processed on the partners' websites. When you use a search widget on Gsirigo, you are redirected to the partner's secure website to complete your booking.
